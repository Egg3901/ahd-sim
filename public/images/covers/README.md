# Scenario & country cover images

Open-source / public-domain photographs used on the landing page and scenario
pickers. Prefer U.S. government works (public domain), UK OGL portraits, and
Wikimedia Commons CC / CC0 images from the relevant campaign cycle.

Per-file Commons title, license, and artist are recorded in `ATTRIBUTION.json`.

Do not hand-edit the JPEGs in place for regeneration — re-fetch from Commons
and re-encode (max width 1000px, JPEG q≈82) so attribution stays accurate.
