Copy rules followed here: no em dashes or en dashes anywhere (only commas,

## Short description (used in search results and the top of the store page)

Run a full election campaign turn by turn. Poll, debate, spend your budget,
react to events, and win on election night, in the US, the UK, or a custom
scenario you build yourself.

## Long description

Electioneer is a turn by turn election campaign simulator. You are not
watching an election happen. You are running one.

Each turn you choose how to spend your time and money: ads, rallies, fundraising,
policy speeches, ground game, and more. Every choice moves your polling
numbers, and the model behind those numbers accounts for regional splits,
demographic groups, and how your opponents are campaigning against you at
the same time.

Debates are their own event. You pick a strategy going in, the exchange
plays out, and a scorecard breaks down who won each moment and why, before
the polling bounce lands.

Random events show up mid campaign: scandals, endorsements, news cycles,
and moments that force you to respond in character or pay a price in the
polls. No two campaigns play out the same way twice.

On election night the results come in region by region, seat by seat or
state by state depending on the country, with a full timeline replay after
the race so you can see exactly where the campaign turned.

Countries included:
- United States: a full presidential race across all 50 states, built on
  the electoral college.
- United Kingdom: every general election from 1979 to 2024 is playable,
  campaigning across the 12 regions plus Northern Ireland, with seats
  driven by regional swing the way electoral votes are driven by state
  polling in the US mode.
- Additional countries and historical elections beyond the US and UK, each
  with their own party systems and regional maps.

Beyond the built in elections, the campaign editor lets you build your own:
set your own candidates, parties, regions, and starting conditions, and
play a scenario nobody else has run before.

A daily challenge gives everyone the same scenario and the same starting
conditions each day, so you can compare your result against how you did
the day before or against friends running the same race.

If you want to understand how a campaign actually works, not just watch
polling numbers move, Electioneer is built for that.
