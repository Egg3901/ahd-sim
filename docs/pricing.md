# Pricing and entitlement policy

## Recommended launch pricing

| Edition | Price | Included content |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Web | Free | Two scenarios and the daily challenge |
| Web packs | $4.99 to $7.99 each | Existing regional scenario packs |
| Web complete collection | $9.99 | Every current paid scenario |
| Direct desktop | $14.99 | Full current game, offline desktop client |
| Steam | $14.99 | Full current game through Steam ownership |
| iOS and Android | $7.99 | Full current game as one non-consumable unlock |

These are one-time prices. There is no subscription because the game does not
yet deliver enough recurring value to justify one.

## Platform rules

- Web and direct desktop builds may use the Lakeside checkout and existing
  `electioneer` entitlements.
- Steam builds must use Steam ownership. They never show Lakeside checkout.
- iOS builds must use an App Store non-consumable purchase and restoration.
- Google Play builds must use Play Billing and restoration.
- A signed-in player may use an existing Lakeside entitlement on another
  platform only where store policy permits and the equivalent content is also
  available through that platform's required purchase system.
- Product IDs, save formats, database keys, and the live URL remain
  `electioneer` for compatibility.

## Launch sequence

1. Keep the open beta free while native clients and analytics are validated.
2. Launch direct desktop at $14.99 with the web complete collection included.
3. Launch mobile at $7.99 after native billing and restore tests pass.
4. Run a bounded web paywall cohort before enabling it for every visitor.
5. Revisit pricing from conversion, refund, retention, and support data.

Store fees, taxes, and regional pricing mean gross price is not net revenue.
The business target must be measured from settled proceeds.
