# Margin of Victory launch plan

## Product promise

**Run the campaign. Change the result.**

Margin of Victory is a turn-based election strategy game about scarce time,
money, staff, momentum, and hard choices. It covers 49 campaigns across six
countries, from 1951 to 2027. The base game and daily challenge are free.
Optional packs add historical campaigns through one-time purchases.

## Best first audience

Start with players already interested in election simulators, political
history, grand strategy, and alternate history. The clearest hook is not
"accurate polling software." It is the fantasy of taking control of a famous
campaign and discovering whether a different strategy could change history.

## Store copy

### Short description

Take command of 49 election campaigns across six countries. Spend scarce time
and money, react to debates and scandals, and find out whether your strategy
can change history.

### Feature bullets

- Run campaigns in the US, UK, France, Germany, Canada, and Australia.
- Make consequential choices about advertising, rallies, fundraising, field
  operations, policy, opposition research, and debate preparation.
- Learn a different electoral system and strategic puzzle in each country.
- Compare scores in a shared daily challenge.
- Build custom campaigns and inspect why you won or lost.
- Play the base game free. Add historical scenario packs with one-time
  purchases. No subscription.

### Calls to action

- Primary: Play free
- Secondary: See all 49 campaigns
- Store: Unlock more elections

## Promotion sequence

1. Invite 25 to 50 strategy and political-history players into a named public
   beta. Ask each player to finish one campaign and answer three questions:
   where they became confused, whether they wanted another run, and which pack
   they would consider buying.
2. Publish short clips built from real gameplay: a close state swing, a debate
   decision, election-night reversals, and the final why-you-won report.
3. Post the daily challenge as a repeatable social format. Use one result card,
   one strategic question, and a direct play link. Avoid generic studio posts.
4. Contact election-game creators, political-history channels, strategy-game
   newsletters, and relevant communities with a playable link and a specific
   scenario suited to their audience.
5. Enable web pack sales only after purchase, refund, and entitlement recovery
   have been exercised end to end in production-safe test transactions.
6. Spend on ads only after organic visitors show that they start campaigns,
   finish them, and return. Until then, paid traffic buys noise rather than
   evidence.

## Minimum launch measurement

Measure the funnel without collecting unnecessary personal data:

- Landing page to campaign start
- Campaign start to first completed turn
- First completed turn to finished campaign
- Finished campaign to second campaign within seven days
- Store viewed, checkout started, checkout completed, and entitlement restored
- Daily challenge participation and result sharing
- Crash-free sessions and support requests per 100 players

Initial decision thresholds for a public marketing push:

- At least 60 percent of starters complete the first turn
- At least 30 percent of starters finish a campaign
- At least 20 percent of finishers begin another campaign within seven days
- At least 99 percent crash-free sessions
- Every test purchase unlocks immediately and restores after a fresh sign-in

These are operating gates, not claims about industry benchmarks. Adjust them
after the first 50 qualified players provide real data.

## Current release gates

The web build is suitable for a limited public beta after the Lakeside site and
account-store rebrand ship. Broad paid marketing should wait for funnel events,
production-safe checkout verification, a complete screenshot set, and a short
trailer.

Desktop release also needs signed installers and update-path testing. Mobile
release needs native billing, restore purchases, signing, physical-device QA,
and store review materials. Steam release needs DLC ownership integration and
Steamworks test-package verification.
